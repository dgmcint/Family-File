Family Keeper

A simple, friendly place to record your family history, in the spirit of the classic genealogy programs. Free for Windows, Mac and Linux.

Download Now GitHub

Features

  • Record people, families, events and sources in one place
  • Clear family and pedigree screens, easy to learn
  • Saves your work as a .fkf family file
  • Your data stays on your computer, with no account and no ads

About

Family Keeper is a free, independent project inspired by the straightforward genealogy programs many families relied on for years, rebuilt to run natively on modern computers.
